01 — INTRODUCTION

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence, commonly called AI, is a technology that allows computers and machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. These tasks include learning, understanding language, recognizing images, solving problems, and making decisions.

02 — APPLICATIONS

Where is AI Used?

🤖

Robotics

AI helps robots understand their surroundings and perform useful tasks.

🏥

Healthcare

AI can help doctors analyze medical information and support diagnosis.

🚗

Transportation

AI is used in navigation systems and research into autonomous vehicles.

🎓

Education

AI can provide personalized learning tools and educational assistance.

03 — TYPES

Types of AI

Narrow AI

AI designed to perform specific tasks, such as voice assistants or recommendation systems.

General AI

A theoretical form of AI that would be able to perform a wide range of intellectual tasks.

Machine Learning

A method where computers learn patterns from data and improve their performance.

04 — THE FUTURE

The Future of AI

Artificial Intelligence is developing rapidly and may transform many areas of science, education, healthcare, transportation, entertainment, and business.
